Santiago eats well and knows it. For this list we let the diners decide: we ranked the restaurants in the guide by their **Google rating** and, when two tie, by their **number of reviews**. Only places with **at least 100 reviews** count, so a perfect five from ten votes cannot beat a 4.8 backed by thousands, and we took **just one location per brand**.

The list that came out is very Santiago: grilled meat, seafood, Dominican cooking, a bit of Mexico and Spain, and two or three spots that belong more to the neighborhood than to the tourist trail. The ratings are from September 2026 and they move: the latest one is on each restaurant's page.

## 1. Puros y Carnes Gurabo

![Puros y Carnes Gurabo](/media/sbglmrtq/puros-y-carnes-gurabo-google.jpg)

**4.9 on Google from more than 250 reviews**, the highest rating in the city. Grilled meats in Gurabo, with a terrace, live music and parking, in a setting made for an unhurried group dinner. It opens only in the evening and only Thursday to Sunday, so it is a weekend plan.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/steakhouse-grill/puros-y-carnes-gurabo/)

## 2. Amigos Seafood Steakhouse

![Amigos Seafood Steakhouse](/media/z10bam1a/amigos-seafood-steakhouse-restaurante-en-santiago-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 8,700 reviews**, the most reviewed restaurant in Santiago. Seafood and steaks on Avenida Salvador Estrella Sadhalá, with a terrace, live music, sports on screen and room for kids. Open every day from noon to 11:30 p.m., and one of the few places that works as well for a family dinner as for a big group.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/seafood/amigos-seafood-steakhouse-restaurante-en-santiago/)

## 3. Restaurante Campuno

![Restaurante Campuno](/media/anngbzze/restaurante-campuno-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 7,100 reviews.** Traditional Dominican cooking in a large, central dining room with a terrace and parking, open every day from 8:00 a.m. It is the place for a Dominican family meal, from breakfast to dinner.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/dominican/restaurante-campuno/)

## 4. Giacomo Restaurante Café

![Giacomo Restaurante Café](/media/emhlnssj/giacomo-restaurante-caf%C3%A9-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 3,600 reviews.** An American-style bistro and pub on Avenida Benito Juárez, open every day from 10:00 a.m. to midnight. It suits a relaxed brunch, a casual dinner or a get-together with friends, and its menu is on the restaurant's page.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/american/giacomo-restaurante-cafe/)

## 5. Sazón MX

![Sazón MX](/media/dlmfqs3m/saz%C3%B3n-mx-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 300 reviews.** Mexican cooking on Calle Salomé Ureña, with a terrace and a setting that works for a dinner for two. On weekdays it opens at 5:00 p.m., on Saturdays and Sundays at noon, and it is closed on Tuesdays.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/mexican/sazon-mx/)

## 6. Restaurante Pascale

![Restaurante Pascale](/media/jsvlhyed/restaurante-pascale-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 250 reviews.** Mediterranean cooking on Avenida Benito Juárez, open every day from noon to midnight. It works for a business lunch as well as for a special dinner, and there is parking.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/mediterranean/restaurante-pascale/)

## 7. La Galería Museo - Cervecería - Tapas

![La Galería Museo - Cervecería - Tapas](/media/w0eo23xx/la-galer%C3%ADa-museo-cervecer%C3%ADa-tapas-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 200 reviews.** Art, craft beer and tapas on Calle 16 de Agosto: a space that is part gallery and part brewpub, sometimes with live music. It opens in the late afternoon and is closed on Tuesdays. A good way to start the night.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/spanish/la-galeria-museo-cerveceria-tapas/)

## 8. La Esquina de Chicue

![La Esquina de Chicue](/media/arjjyorn/la-esquina-de-chicue-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 200 reviews.** Fast food for a casual lunch or dinner, with a terrace and parking. Open Monday to Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., on Saturdays only until 4:00 p.m., and closed on Sundays.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/fast-food/la-esquina-de-chicue/)

## 9. Chicharrón El Patio

![Chicharrón El Patio](/media/5cshxnv4/chicharron-el-patio-google.jpg)

**4.8 from 150 reviews.** Chicharrón and traditional Dominican food in a no-frills setting, with a terrace and parking. Mind the hours: **it only opens on Fridays and Saturdays**, so it is a weekend outing worth planning ahead.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/dominican/chicharron-el-patio/)

## 10. Thesaurus Café

![Thesaurus Café](/media/g0vaqab2/thesaurus-caf%C3%A9-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 130 reviews.** On the second floor of Centro Plaza Internacional, on Avenida Juan Pablo Duarte, an air-conditioned café and restaurant for a relaxed meal with family or friends. Open every day from 11:00 a.m.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/other/thesaurus-cafe/)

## Before you go

- Each page shows the Google rating and, when there is one, the rating from QueHacerRD users, side by side and never mixed.
- Check the hours on the page before heading out: Puros y Carnes opens Thursday to Sunday evenings, and Chicharrón El Patio only on Fridays and Saturdays.
- Amigos and Campuno are the busiest: on weekends it pays to arrive early.
