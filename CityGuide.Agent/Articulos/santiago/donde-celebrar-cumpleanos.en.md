Planning a birthday is solving a puzzle: enough room for everyone, a menu that works for the uncle who only eats steak and the vegetarian cousin, a place where you can talk, and a bill that is not a shock. Santiago makes it easy, because eating out in a group is a habit here and restaurants are built for it.

These five are among the most reviewed and best rated in the whole city, and all five welcome groups. The golden rule for any of them: **book several days ahead**, say how many you are, and ask about the cake policy, because every house has its own.

## Square One

![Square One](/media/kfhnnla4/square-one-google.jpg)

The most reviewed restaurant in Santiago, on Avenida Estrella Sadhalá across from PUCMM university. Its biggest strength for a birthday is the menu: huge, with options for everyone, from mains to quesadillas, and a dessert section that almost makes bringing a cake unnecessary. Plenty of space and a modern feel.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/other/square-one/)

## Amigos Seafood Steakhouse

![Amigos Seafood Steakhouse](/media/z10bam1a/amigos-seafood-steakhouse-restaurante-en-santiago-google.jpg)

Seafood and steaks with one of the highest ratings in the city. This is the pick when the birthday calls for a dinner with a bit more ceremony: sharing plates in the middle of the table, a good drinks list and service that is used to big parties.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/seafood/amigos-seafood-steakhouse-restaurante-en-santiago/)

## Restaurante Campuno

![Restaurante Campuno](/media/anngbzze/restaurante-campuno-google.jpg)

Dominican home-style cooking, the kind that gets three generations to agree at the same table. With thousands of reviews and a very high rating, it is the perfect plan for a Sunday family birthday, grandparents included, with a long after-lunch chat.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/dominican/restaurante-campuno/)

## Giacomo Restaurante Café

![Giacomo Restaurante Café](/media/emhlnssj/giacomo-restaurante-caf%C3%A9-google.jpg)

A restaurant and café in one, which makes it ideal for smaller celebrations: a birthday brunch, lunch with friends or a quiet dinner. Its menu is published in the guide, so you can start deciding before you arrive.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/american/giacomo-restaurante-cafe/)

## Ocio GastroPub

![Ocio GastroPub](/media/lsadpoh2/ocio-gastropub-santiago-google.png)

For the birthday that starts with dinner and ends with a toast. A colorful gastropub with a terrace, cocktails and a young crowd that livens up after dark. If your group sings happy birthday at full volume, nobody here will look twice.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santiago/restaurants/other/ocio-gastropub-santiago/)

## Quick checklist before you book

- Book early and confirm the headcount the day before.
- Ask whether you can bring a cake and whether there is a serving fee.
- Ask for a set menu or sharing plates if the group is large: splitting the bill gets easier.
- Mention it is a birthday; many places have a little something for the guest of honor.
