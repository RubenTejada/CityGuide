This guide lists more than a thousand restaurants in Santo Domingo, and nearly all of them have someone ready to vouch for them. For this list we let the diners decide: we ranked the restaurants by their **Google rating** and, when two tie, by their **number of reviews**. Only places with **at least 200 reviews** count, so a perfect five from ten votes cannot beat a 4.9 backed by thousands, and we took **just one location per brand**.

The result says a lot about how the capital eats: few white tablecloths and plenty of casual spots that do one thing very well. Tacos, pizza, burgers, shawarma, seafood and coffee. The ratings are from September 2026 and they move: the latest one is on each restaurant's page.

## 1. Taconazo

![Taconazo](/media/hrcpgze4/taconazo.jpg)

**4.9 on Google from more than 9,400 reviews**, by far the most reviewed place on the list. Tacos and Mexican food on Avenida Tiradentes in Naco, **open 24 hours a day, every day**: it works as well for a quick lunch as for the craving after a night out. Delivery and WiFi available.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santo-domingo/restaurants/mexican/taconazo/)

## 2. Granier Gazcue

![Granier Gazcue](/media/ejxdyy4q/granier-gazcue.jpg)

**4.9 from more than 1,300 reviews.** A Spanish bakery and pastry shop on Calle Ángel Perdomo in Gazcue, for breakfast, brunch or a coffee break. It opens at 6:30 a.m. Monday to Saturday (8:00 a.m. on Sundays) and stays open until 8:30 p.m.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santo-domingo/restaurants/breakfast-brunch/granier-gazcue/)

## 3. Italia Mia - Pizza & Burgers

![Italia Mia - Pizza & Burgers](/media/mlhg4vmi/italia-mia-pizza-burgers.jpg)

**4.9 from more than 1,300 reviews**, and the only place on the list in Santo Domingo Este. Pizza and burgers in a laid-back spot that suits a night out with friends or an order for delivery. It opens in the afternoon and is closed on Tuesdays.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santo-domingo/restaurants/italian/italia-mia-pizza-burgers/)

## 4. Anthony's Coal Fired Pizza Naco

![Anthony's Coal Fired Pizza Naco](/media/no1j4jlq/anthony-s-coal-fired-pizza-naco.jpg)

**4.9 from nearly 700 reviews.** Coal-fired pizza on Avenida Tiradentes, in a setting made for family dinners or a meal with friends. It opens at 8:00 a.m. and serves until midnight on Fridays and Saturdays. Parking available. Its Piantini location is rated 4.8 as well.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santo-domingo/restaurants/italian/anthonys-coal-fired-pizza-naco/)

## 5. Smash Burger - Arroyo Hondo

![Smash Burger - Arroyo Hondo](/media/yhudxvqk/smash-burger-arroyo-hondo.jpg)

**4.9 from about 700 reviews.** American-style burgers in Arroyo Hondo, open every day from 11:30 a.m. to midnight. There is parking, and it is one of those places that handles lunch as well as the last stop of the night.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santo-domingo/restaurants/fast-food/smash-burger-arroyo-hondo/)

## 6. Lúpulos Café

![Lúpulos Café](/media/cyhbthyt/l%C3%BApulos-caf%C3%A9.jpg)

**4.9 from more than 400 reviews.** A quiet café on Calle Padre Fantino Falco, with air conditioning and WiFi, where people come for breakfast, to work on a laptop or to talk without hurry. Open every day from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santo-domingo/restaurants/breakfast-brunch/lupulos-cafe/)

## 7. Shawarma La Turkita Grill

![Shawarma La Turkita Grill](/media/os5mtwuk/shawarma-la-turkita-grill.jpg)

**4.9 from more than 300 reviews.** Shawarma and Lebanese food on Avenida Rómulo Betancourt, for a casual dinner with friends. Open Tuesday to Sunday from noon, until midnight most days; closed on Mondays.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santo-domingo/restaurants/middle-eastern/shawarma-la-turkita-grill/)

## 8. Coctel del Mar

![Coctel del Mar](/media/msrcdfno/coctel-del-mar.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 2,500 reviews**, the top seafood spot in this ranking. It is on Avenida Charles de Gaulle in Santo Domingo Este, has parking and air conditioning, and opens every day at 11:00 a.m. A classic for a family meal of fish and seafood.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santo-domingo/restaurants/seafood/coctel-del-mar/)

## 9. Calcalí Coffee - Naco

![Calcalí Coffee - Naco](/media/sl1czgwh/calcal%C3%AD-coffee-naco.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 1,100 reviews.** Specialty coffee and brunch at Plaza Paseo del Prado in Naco. It opens at 7:00 a.m. Tuesday to Sunday, so it works for breakfast before work as well as for a casual meeting. Its menu is on the restaurant's page.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santo-domingo/restaurants/breakfast-brunch/calcali-coffee-naco/)

## 10. ZOLA

![ZOLA](/media/z02hpmgu/zola.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 900 reviews.** Italian cooking on Calle Las Mercedes, right in the Colonial City, open every day from 12:30 p.m. to midnight. It is the best pick on the list for a romantic dinner or a celebration, and a good way to end a walk through the colonial district.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/santo-domingo/restaurants/italian/zola/)

## Before you go

- Each page shows the Google rating and, when there is one, the rating from QueHacerRD users, side by side and never mixed.
- Several of these places are small and fill up at weekend peak hours: arrive early or order delivery.
- Check the hours on the page before heading out: Italia Mia is closed on Tuesdays, and La Turkita and Calcalí on Mondays.
