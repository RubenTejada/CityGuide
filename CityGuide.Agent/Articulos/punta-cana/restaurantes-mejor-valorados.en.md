In Punta Cana people eat at the hotels, in the plazas of Bávaro and along the boulevards that lead to the beach. For this list we let the diners decide: we ranked the restaurants in the guide by their **Google rating** and, when two tie, by their **number of reviews**. Only places with **at least 100 reviews** count, so a perfect five from ten votes cannot beat a 4.9 backed by thousands.

One thing needs saying: five of the top seven spots are restaurants at the same resort, Lopesan Costa Bávaro. To keep the list useful for anyone eating out in the area, we took **just one restaurant per brand or per hotel**, and the resort's others are mentioned in its entry. The ratings are from September 2026 and they move: the latest one is on each restaurant's page.

## 1. El Charro at Lopesan Costa Bávaro Resort

![El Charro at Lopesan Costa Bávaro Resort](/media/nsljeihb/el-charro-at-lopesan-costa-b%C3%A1varo-resort-google.jpg)

**5.0 on Google from more than 3,600 reviews**: a perfect five backed by thousands of opinions, which you almost never see. Mexican cooking inside Lopesan Costa Bávaro, for a dinner for two or with the family. It is not the only star at the resort: **La Bohème** (European, 4.9), **Mareas** (international, 4.9), **Inari** (Asian, 4.9) and **El Asador** (grill, 4.8) also outrank nearly the whole city. If you are not staying there, check with the hotel whether it takes bookings from outside guests before you go.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/mexican/el-charro-at-lopesan-costa-bavaro-resort/)

## 2. Chinois Restaurante - Punta Cana

![Chinois Restaurante - Punta Cana](/media/nltfi5kf/chinois-restaurante-punta-cana-google.jpg)

**4.9 from more than 1,600 reviews.** Chinese food with Cantonese specialties, dim sum and dumplings, on Calle Paseo San Juan. It has air conditioning, welcomes kids and is open every day from noon to 11:00 p.m.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/chinese/chinois-restaurante-punta-cana/)

## 3. The Scene BlueMall

![The Scene BlueMall](/media/uppnnvvq/the-scene-bluemall-sitio-del-lugar-websitecreator-blob-core-windows-net.png)

**4.9 from more than 150 reviews.** Contemporary cooking at BlueMall, on Avenida Ted Kheel, with live music. It opens at 8:00 a.m. and on Fridays and Saturdays stays open until 2:00 a.m., so it works for dinner and for staying on.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/other/the-scene-bluemall/)

## 4. Market DR

![Market DR](/media/1ftg12dv/market-dr-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 350 reviews.** A restaurant and bar, also at BlueMall, that works for a casual lunch or an evening get-together. Open every day from 9:00 a.m., with parking.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/other/market-dr/)

## 5. Ventanas Bar & Restaurant

![Ventanas Bar & Restaurant](/media/awqnnlhk/ventanas-bar-restaurant-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 300 reviews.** Seafood, pizza and grilled dishes at kilometer 28 of the Boulevard Turístico del Este, with a terrace and easy access by car. Open every day; on weekends from 9:00 a.m. A good choice for a family meal.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/pizzerias/ventanas-bar-restaurant/)

## 6. Bohemian Tapas and Wine Bar

![Bohemian Tapas and Wine Bar](/media/f31ft3f1/bohemian-tapas-and-wine-bar-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 170 reviews.** Tapas and wine on Boulevard Fishing Lodge, in an intimate setting made for couples and small groups. Open Monday to Saturday from 5:00 p.m. and closed on Sundays.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/spanish/bohemian-tapas-and-wine-bar/)

## 7. Maestro Downtown Beer Garden

![Maestro Downtown Beer Garden](/media/3yjhjj5m/maestro-downtown-beer-garden-google.jpg)

**4.8 from nearly 140 reviews.** An open-air gastropub with a wide range of craft beers, for a relaxed night out with friends. Open every day at 5:00 p.m. until past midnight. Its menu is on the restaurant's page.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/other/maestro-downtown-beer-garden/)

## 8. Castaways

![Castaways](/media/zu3m05sl/castaways-sitio-del-lugar-castawaysbistro-com.jpg)

**4.7 from more than 550 reviews.** Next to the El Dorado casino in Los Corales and close to Playa Turquesa, a restaurant with a varied menu and a beach feel. Open every day from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., so it works for breakfast too.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/other/castaways/)

## 9. La Nena Punta Cana

![La Nena Punta Cana](/media/lekfvkkv/la-nena-punta-cana-google.jpg)

**4.7 from nearly 480 reviews.** Dominican food from breakfast to dinner, from 7:00 a.m. every day; on Saturdays until 2:00 a.m. Its menu is on the restaurant's page.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/dominican/la-nena-punta-cana/)

## 10. Lolita Restaurante Punta Cana

![Lolita Restaurante Punta Cana](/media/lvomzwcx/lolita-restaurante-punta-cana-sitio-del-lugar-lolitarest-wordpress-com.png)

**4.7 from more than 440 reviews.** At Plaza Cueva Taína on Avenida Estados Unidos, a varied menu in a cozy setting that suits a dinner for two. Open from noon; on Sundays it closes at 6:00 p.m.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/other/lolita-restaurante-punta-cana/)

## Before you go

- Each page shows the Google rating and, when there is one, the rating from QueHacerRD users, side by side and never mixed.
- Hotel restaurants may be reserved for guests or require a booking: check before you go.
- Distances in Punta Cana are long: look at the map on each page before heading out, and keep in mind that Bohemian is closed on Sundays.
