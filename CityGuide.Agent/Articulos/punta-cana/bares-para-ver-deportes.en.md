Being on vacation does not mean missing the game. Every week Punta Cana brings together fans of the NFL, the NBA, hockey, Dominican baseball and European soccer, and the area has answered with sports bars where you can watch almost everything at once.

These five are listed on Google as good for watching sports and are among the most reviewed of their kind. They are spread across Bávaro, Los Corales and Downtown. One tip: if the game you want is not one of the big ones, **message them on Instagram or WhatsApp first** to confirm they will show it.

## Hard Rock Cafe Punta Cana

![Hard Rock Cafe Punta Cana](/media/3mwgtqfu/hard-rock-cafe-punta-cana-google-1.jpg)

At the Downtown plaza, on the Bulevar Turístico del Este. Screens, a terrace, burgers and ribs, surrounded by rock memorabilia. It is the comfortable choice for big groups and families: air conditioning, a broad menu and plaza parking.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/american/hard-rock-cafe-punta-cana/)

## Strikers Sports Bar

![Strikers Sports Bar](/media/igflo2je/strikers-sports-bar-google-1.jpg)

A sports bar in every sense: screens everywhere, a terrace and a late-night atmosphere, open 24 hours. It is inside the Barceló Bávaro Palace complex, so if you are not staying there, ask about access conditions beforehand.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/bars-clubs/strikers-sports-bar/)

## Herman 311 Bar & Restaurant

![Herman 311 Bar & Restaurant](/media/o1fdwxfz/herman-311-bar-restaurant-google-1.jpg)

At Plaza Victoriana, across from the Gran Bávaro Princess. One of the best-loved restaurants outside the hotel circuit, with good seafood cooking, live music and screens for the game. The pick for those who want to watch the match over a proper meal, not just bar snacks.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/seafood/herman-311-bar-restaurant/)

## Xtra Time Sports Bar

![Xtra Time Sports Bar](/media/2thfasyv/sports-bar-xtra-time-google-1.jpg)

On Avenida Palladium, in Bávaro. High-definition screens, a terrace and parking. It is a bar built for game day: you arrive, pick a screen and order a bucket of beers.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/bars-clubs/sports-bar-xtra-time/)

## 2Dudes Sport Bar

![2Dudes Sport Bar](/media/t30gjhws/2dudes-sport-bar-google-1.jpg)

At Plaza El Dorado, in Los Corales, the most walkable part of Bávaro. Small, very well rated, and with the promise that you can watch every sport at once. It has a terrace and a crowd of expats and tourists who already know each other by name.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/bars-clubs/2dudes-sport-bar/)

## So you do not miss the game

- Confirm the game and the local time: Punta Cana does not switch to daylight saving time.
- For the Super Bowl, finals and derbies, book a table or arrive very early.
- If you are drinking, take a taxi back: distances between areas are long and the roads are dark at night.
