In Punta Cana there is only one view, and it is unbeatable: the Caribbean Sea in every shade of turquoise there is. The question is not whether you want to eat looking at it, but where. And the best answer is usually outside the hotel buffet.

We picked five oceanfront restaurants that are among the most reviewed in the area, from Bávaro to the marina at Puntacana Resort. Two tips apply to all of them: **book ahead, especially for dinner**, and if you are staying at an all-inclusive, arrange the taxi there and back before you leave. Lunch has the better light and the better sea color; dinner, the better temperature.

## Jellyfish Restaurant

![Jellyfish Restaurant](/media/azvfu4tr/jellyfish-restaurant-google-1.jpg)

The most photographed restaurant in Punta Cana. Its two-story wooden structure, open to the sea on the sand at Bávaro, has become a local icon. The menu revolves around fresh seafood and Caribbean cooking, with lobster as the star, and there is live music some days. It is also the most reviewed spot on this list, so book early.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/seafood/jellyfish-restaurant/)

## Playa Blanca Restaurant

![Playa Blanca Restaurant](/media/gythy25t/playa-blanca-restaurant-google-1.png)

Inside Puntacana Resort & Club, on one of the calmest beaches in the area. Caribbean cooking, seafood and international options, in a relaxed setting where kids are welcome. It makes a good full-day plan: a long lunch, a calm sea and no rush.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/dominican/playa-blanca-restaurant/)

## Pearl Beach Club

![Pearl Beach Club](/media/3oae0nkv/pearl-beach-club-google-1.jpg)

A Bávaro beach club for spending the whole day: a restaurant facing the sea, music and a beach vibe that picks up in the afternoon. Seafood, cocktails and weekend brunch. It works just as well for a meal for two as for a group in a party mood.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/seafood/pearl-beach-club/)

## Huracán Beach Club

![Huracán Beach Club](/media/xcoprc3r/huracan-beach-club-google-1.jpg)

A Bávaro beach classic, with your feet practically in the sand. An Italian and European menu with seafood, pasta and something for everyone, live music and its own parking, which is gold in that area. A casual atmosphere, perfect for showing up in a swimsuit after some beach time.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/italian/huracan-beach-club/)

## La Yola

![La Yola](/media/ujfmp0cp/la-yola-google-1.jpg)

Punta Cana's romantic table. The restaurant is built on stilts, literally over the water, at the Puntacana Resort marina. Mediterranean-inspired cooking with an emphasis on seafood, a terrace and live music some nights. For an anniversary or a special dinner it is hard to beat; ask for a table at the edge.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/punta-cana/restaurants/seafood/la-yola/)

## Before you go

- Book in advance and ask for a front-row table.
- Bring cash or confirm cards are accepted; some beach clubs charge a minimum spend for beach beds.
- The midday sun is strong even in the shade: sunscreen and sunglasses.
