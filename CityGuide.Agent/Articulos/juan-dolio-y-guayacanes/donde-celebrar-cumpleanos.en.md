A birthday in Juan Dolio is usually a whole weekend: the family rents an apartment, friends arrive on Saturday and the celebration revolves around one long meal. The town is small, but it has more than enough restaurants to rise to the occasion.

These five are among the most reviewed in Juan Dolio and Guayacanes, and all of them welcome groups. Since they are mid-sized places, the rule matters more here than in the city: **book days ahead and say how many you are**, especially if the birthday falls on a long weekend.

## Paladart

![Paladart](/media/gbrnpdza/paladart-restaurante-parrillada-argentina-cocina-mediterr%C3%A1nea-google-1.jpg)

Argentine grill and Mediterranean cooking on the Bulevar de Juan Dolio. Grilled cuts to share, a terrace and service used to big tables. One of the best rated in town, and a safe bet when the carnivores in the group are calling the shots.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/steakhouse-grill/paladart-restaurante-parrillada-argentinacocina-mediterranea/)

## El Concón Juan Dolio

![El Concón Juan Dolio](/media/qiml2ca3/el-conc%C3%B3n-juan-dolio-google-1.jpg)

Seafood and Dominican flavor on Carretera Nueva, with a terrace and a family atmosphere. Its name pays tribute to the crispy rice at the bottom of the pot, the best part, and the menu lives up to it. Ideal for a birthday with grandparents, uncles and kids at the same table.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/dominican/el-concon-juan-dolio/)

## Soya Azul Restaurant

![Soya Azul Restaurant](/media/4g3jpkq5/soya-azul-restaurant-google-1.jpg)

In Guayacanes, the neighboring fishing village, with a laid-back setting by the beach. A terrace, brunch and pets allowed. This is the plan for a daytime celebration: a long lunch beside the sea and zero formality.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/mediterranean/soya-azul-restaurant/)

## Sal Marina Restaurant

![Sal Marina Restaurant](/media/ooxpmwnl/sal-marina-restaurant-google-1.jpg)

At Plaza Colonia Tropical, with a menu that blends seafood and Caribbean cooking, vegetarian and gluten-free options, and a kids' menu. The detail that makes it special for a celebration: live violin in the evenings. Closed on Mondays.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/seafood/sal-marina-restaurant/)

## Restaurante Da Oreste

![Restaurante Da Oreste](/media/vaggcdic/restaurante-da-oreste-google-1.jpg)

Authentic Italian cooking on Calle Principal, with a terrace and a homey welcome. A cozy setting, the kind that suits an intimate birthday more than a big party. Pasta, good wine and lingering conversation.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/italian/restaurante-da-oreste/)

## Quick checklist before you book

- Book early; on long weekends Juan Dolio fills up.
- Ask whether you can bring a cake and whether there is a serving fee.
- Order sharing plates if the group is large: splitting the bill gets easier.
- Mention it is a birthday; many places have a little something for the guest of honor.
