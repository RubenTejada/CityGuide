Juan Dolio y Guayacanes is a beach town, and it eats like one: on the boulevard, in the plazas along the Carretera Nueva and at tables a few steps from the sand. For this list we let the diners decide: we ranked the restaurants in the guide by their **Google rating** and, when two tie, by their **number of reviews**.

Since it is a much smaller place than the capital, the minimum is **50 reviews** rather than a hundred: enough to keep a perfect five from ten votes off the top, without leaving out the neighborhood spots. The ratings are from September 2026 and they move: the latest one is on each restaurant's page.

## 1. Restaurante Sabor Tricolor con Aroma de Venezuela

![Restaurante Sabor Tricolor con Aroma de Venezuela](/media/p2il1lek/restaurante-sabor-tricolor-con-aroma-de-venezuela-google.jpg)

**4.9 on Google from more than 100 reviews**, the highest rating in town. Traditional Venezuelan cooking on the Juan Dolio boulevard, in a family-friendly, air-conditioned setting. It opens at 8:00 a.m., so it works for breakfast as well as dinner; closed on Tuesdays.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/other/restaurante-sabor-tricolor-con-aroma-de-venezuela/)

## 2. New Wave Gastro Bar

![New Wave Gastro Bar](/media/lxsl1rjt/new-wave-gastro-bar-google.jpg)

**4.8 from more than 130 reviews.** Contemporary cooking at Plaza La Llave on the Carretera Nueva, open every day from 8:00 a.m. A modern spot for a relaxed lunch or dinner, with parking.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/other/new-wave-gastro-bar/)

## 3. 1 Vida Restaurante Bar

![1 Vida Restaurante Bar](/media/a34dlxb4/1-vida-restaurante-bar-google.jpg)

**4.8 from 50 reviews.** At Marina 72 in Guayacanes, a varied menu in a laid-back setting, for an outing with friends or family. Open Tuesday to Sunday and closed on Mondays.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/other/1-vida-restaurante-bar/)

## 4. Plaza Leche y Miel

![Plaza Leche y Miel](/media/o3dn2grk/plaza-leche-y-miel-google.jpg)

**4.7 from more than 160 reviews.** A café with a terrace right on the boulevard, for breakfast or an afternoon snack after the beach. Open Tuesday to Sunday from the morning, closing at 7:00 p.m. on weekdays and at 9:00 p.m. Friday to Sunday.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/breakfast-brunch/plaza-leche-y-miel/)

## 5. Coral Bistro

![Coral Bistro](/media/dzkbjhpg/coral-bistro-google.jpg)

**4.6 from more than 75 reviews.** Local and international cooking by the sea on the boulevard, with a terrace and a setting fit for a special dinner. Open Tuesday to Sunday from noon.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/mediterranean/coral-bistro/)

## 6. Da Martina e Beppe

![Da Martina e Beppe](/media/3cug5igp/da-martina-e-beppe-google.jpg)

**4.6 from more than 50 reviews.** Italian cooking in an air-conditioned spot with parking. Mind the hours: it is open Tuesday to Sunday **from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.**, so it is a lunch plan, not a dinner one.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/italian/da-martina-e-beppe/)

## 7. Restaurante El Mesón Español

![Restaurante El Mesón Español](/media/w02b5wah/restaurante-el-mes%C3%B3n-espa%C3%B1ol-google.jpg)

**4.5 from nearly 1,700 reviews**, the most reviewed restaurant in Juan Dolio. Spanish cooking with a sea view on the Carretera Nueva, a terrace and room for groups and families. Open every day from 11:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.; it also tops our list of [restaurants with the best views](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/articles/the-restaurants-with-the-best-views-in-juan-dolio/).

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/spanish/restaurante-el-meson-espanol/)

## 8. Paladart Restaurante

![Paladart Restaurante](/media/ui4p3h4q/paladart-restaurante-parrillada-argentina-cocina-mediterr%C3%A1nea-google.jpg)

**4.5 from more than 1,200 reviews.** Argentine grill and Mediterranean cooking on the boulevard, across from Banco Popular, with a terrace and a setting for dinners with friends and celebrations. Open Tuesday to Sunday from 11:00 a.m.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/steakhouse-grill/paladart-restaurante-parrillada-argentinacocina-mediterranea/)

## 9. Sal Marina Restaurant

![Sal Marina Restaurant](/media/caxg4fxl/sal-marina-restaurant-google.jpg)

**4.5 from nearly 600 reviews.** Fish and seafood at Plaza Colonia Tropical on the Carretera Nueva, with a terrace and live music at times. Open Tuesday to Sunday from noon.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/seafood/sal-marina-restaurant/)

## 10. Restaurante Da Oreste

![Restaurante Da Oreste](/media/t1ehzf5g/restaurante-da-oreste-google.jpg)

**4.5 from more than 380 reviews.** Italian cooking on Calle Principal, with a terrace and a setting for a meal for two or with friends. On weekdays it closes between lunch and dinner, and it does not open on Tuesdays: check the hours on its page.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/italian/restaurante-da-oreste/)

## Before you go

- Each page shows the Google rating and, when there is one, the rating from QueHacerRD users, side by side and never mixed.
- In Juan Dolio many restaurants close on Mondays (and Sabor Tricolor and Da Oreste on Tuesdays): check the hours before heading out.
- On Sundays they fill up with visitors from the capital: book ahead or arrive before one o'clock.
