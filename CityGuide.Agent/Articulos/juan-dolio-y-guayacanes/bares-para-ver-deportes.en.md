Let us be honest: people come to Juan Dolio for the beach. But playoff weekends exist, the Estrellas Orientales play a few minutes away, in San Pedro de Macorís, and there is always someone in the group who will not skip the game. For that day, the town has few options, but good ones.

We only include places that truly have screens and a game-day atmosphere, so this list is short: three stops. As in any small town, **it pays to message ahead** to confirm they will show the game you want.

## 365 Sports Bar

![365 Sports Bar](/media/yhilcyba/365-sports-bar-google-1.png)

The town's sports bar, on Calle El Tanque. Screens, a terrace, fast food and delivery, with a schedule that goes beyond sports: karaoke and live DJs some days of the week. It still has few reviews, but every one of them is five stars.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/fast-food/365-sports-bar/)

## 365 American Grill

![365 American Grill](/media/hu4fc4wb/365-american-grill-google-1.png)

The big brother, right next door. American grill cooking, live music, a terrace and screens for the game, with more of a restaurant feel. Kids and pets are welcome, so it is the pick when the plan is watching the match with the family.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/american/365-american-grill/)

## Giulia's Cafe Sport Bar & Restaurant

![Giulia's Cafe Sport Bar & Restaurant](/media/b1ibss3u/giulia-s-cafe-sport-bar-restaurant-google-1.jpg)

A family-run Italian café that doubles as a sports bar. Breakfast, lasagna, tiramisu and gelato, with a terrace and the beach very close. The most reviewed of the three, and the perfect place for an afternoon game with an espresso instead of a beer.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/other/giulias-cafe-sport-bar-restaurant/)

## So you do not miss the game

- Confirm the game on Instagram or WhatsApp; in a small town, whoever arrives first picks what is on.
- During the winter baseball season, October to January, Estrellas games get the whole area going.
- Everything is close: from almost any apartment in Juan Dolio you are five minutes away.
