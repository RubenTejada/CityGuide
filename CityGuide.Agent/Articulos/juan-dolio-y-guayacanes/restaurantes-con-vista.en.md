Juan Dolio is the closest stretch of sea to the capital: less than an hour from Santo Domingo, the highway hugs the coast and a beach town appears, with miles of sand, apartment towers and a row of restaurants facing the Caribbean. Nobody comes here to eat in a hurry.

We picked five oceanfront restaurants among the most reviewed in town. The ideal plan is what people from the capital do on Sundays: **arrive early, swim, and have a long lunch**. Weekends get busy, so book ahead or arrive before one.

## Restaurante El Mesón Español

![Restaurante El Mesón Español](/media/kxqhtjsx/restaurante-el-mes%C3%B3n-espa%C3%B1ol-google-1.jpg)

The most reviewed restaurant in Juan Dolio. Proper Spanish cooking, with paella and tapas in the lead, in a dining room with a terrace and a sea view. It is the classic people come back to: sharing portions, a family atmosphere and weekend brunch.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/spanish/restaurante-el-meson-espanol/)

## Nico's Beach Lounge

![Nico's Beach Lounge](/media/3zfgudwe/nico-s-beach-lounge-google-1.jpg)

Seafood by the sea with a beach-lounge feel: a terrace, mellow music and the water a few steps away. It is pet friendly and good with kids, so it works for the whole group. Order the catch of the day and stay to watch the afternoon fade.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/seafood/nicos-beach-lounge/)

## Oceanna Social Club

![Oceanna Social Club](/media/pgin33wk/oceanna-social-club-google-1.jpg)

A modern space on Carretera Nueva, with a terrace, a varied menu and brunch. It is one of the places that best combines food, cocktails and atmosphere, and a favorite among those who own apartments in the area.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/other/oceanna-social-club/)

## Ristorante El Sueño

![Ristorante El Sueño](/media/lb3a3qsb/ristorante-el-sue%C3%B1o-google-1.jpg)

Italian cooking facing the Caribbean, on Calle Principal. Pasta, pizza and fish on a terrace where the breeze does the air conditioning. Juan Dolio has a long tradition of Italian restaurants, and this is one of the best loved.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/italian/ristorante-el-sueno/)

## La Concha del Mar

![La Concha del Mar](/media/vxkfen5w/la-concha-del-mar-cucine-2015-antiguo-octopus-bay-restaurant-google-1.jpg)

Seafood by the sea on Calle Principal, in the former Octopus Bay spot. A terrace, a laid-back vibe, a menu for everyone and dogs allowed. A good stop for a lunch with no clock after the beach.

[See hours, map and photos](/en/juan-dolio-y-guayacanes/restaurants/seafood/la-concha-del-mar-cucine-2015-antiguo-octopus-bay-restaurant/)

## Before you go

- Saturdays and Sundays are in high demand: book or arrive before one.
- On weekdays Juan Dolio is much quieter, and some restaurants close one day a week: check the hours on their page.
- Almost everything is on Calle Principal or Carretera Nueva; it is a few minutes' drive end to end.
